Dayline

About Dayline

Dayline is an independent iPhone app that brings your day into one clear timeline.

What it does

Dayline shows the places you went and the routes you took, next to the journal entries, photos and voice notes you add. You can look at a day, week, month or year, and search your own journal with a question or a voice clip. You can set your own schedule, so the timeline follows your day.

Why we built it

Most of what makes a day worth remembering is scattered across a map, a camera roll and a notes app. Dayline puts it in one place and keeps it private: your journal, photos, videos and location history stay on your iPhone, and search runs on your device.

Where it stands

Dayline is in beta and still changing. The guide on this site follows the current beta, and the Privacy policy and Terms of Service describe how it works today.
