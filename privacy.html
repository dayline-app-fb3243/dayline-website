Privacy policy

Effective October 2, 2026

Dayline is a day planner and personal timeline for iPhone. This policy explains what the current beta collects, where it is stored, and the choices you have.

What stays on your device

Dayline stores your journal text, recordings, videos, photo references and thumbnails, photo search words, location samples, visits, routines, day scores, preferences, chosen background and avatar locally on your iPhone. The audio of your voice notes and recordings stays on your device and is not uploaded. Your avatar is stored locally.

Local summaries may be available to Dayline widgets, Siri and the iPhone's Spotlight search. Sign-in session credentials are kept in the iPhone's Keychain. Search conversations shown on screen are also kept on your device.

What is stored in your Dayline account

When you create an account, Dayline stores some information with our account service, Supabase. This is tied to your account so it can be restored when you sign in again:

  • Account details: your email address, account ID, sign-in provider and sessions.
  • Profile: your name and name components, and a marker that your profile is complete.
  • Your schedule: the account-linked schedule settings you enter, such as sleep and wake times, step goals and saved places.
  • Saved search conversations: the questions you ask, the answers, and result metadata such as the places or items referenced.

Voice recordings are not uploaded; only text metadata about a saved conversation is synced. Your journal entries, photos, videos and location history are not synced to the account in this build. When you sign in on a phone, Dayline restores your schedule and saved conversations from your account.

Signing in

You can sign in with Apple, with Google, or with your email address and a one-time code. Email codes are delivered through Resend, which processes your email address and the code. Apple or Google sign-in sends the identity tokens needed to sign you in and may supply your name and email; Google sign-in may also supply a profile picture, which the app downloads to your device.

Permissions and how we use them

You control access through iOS Settings. Dayline uses permissions for the following features:

  • Location: builds your places, routes and routines using visits, significant movement and background location updates. Your selected recording interval is not a guarantee of exact sampling times; iOS delivery affects recording.
  • Photos: reads the photos you allow, including dates and saved locations, to add them to your timeline and optionally index them for search. It stores local thumbnails and detected words. Photo analysis runs on your device in batches; cloud-only assets are skipped until locally available. A person-detection heuristic tries to skip prominent-person photos but can make mistakes.
  • Microphone and speech recognition: records journal and search voice clips only while recording. Transcription uses on-device recognition and does not fall back to remote speech recognition.
  • Health, motion and Reminders: reads steps, distance, workouts, motion signals for sleep and wake estimates, and reminder counts. Dayline does not write to Health or Reminders.
  • Contacts: reads permitted names, email addresses and phone numbers for people selection on your device. Contacts are not uploaded to your account.
  • Notifications: schedules local reminders, not server push notifications.

AI and photo processing

Search interpretation and voice-note titles use Apple's on-device models where available. Photo classification, text recognition and face detection use Apple's Vision framework on your device. Dayline does not send these inputs to a cloud language model in this beta.

Maps and places

Maps and place features use Apple services. Coordinates, place-search text and route endpoints may be sent to Apple for maps, nearby places, reverse-geocoding, autocomplete and routes, so location information does not always remain only on your phone. An optional Google Places opening-hours lookup can send place-search text and a location bias when used.

Feedback and support

Feedback opens an email draft addressed to daylineappsupport@gmail.com. You review it and choose whether to send it. Search feedback can include a shortened question and answer in the subject, and Report a Concern includes your written concern and the full question and answer. Your sending email address and anything else you include are received by support. Thumbs-up/down ratings are not uploaded.

No ads, no selling

Dayline does not include advertising or third-party tracking in the app, and we do not sell your personal information. Account, sign-in, mapping and email providers process the information needed for the features above under their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can change permissions at any time in iOS Settings. Profile > Your data signs you out and removes the app's local database, Spotlight index, saved voice and video files, background, avatar and preferences from your phone. It does not delete your Dayline account or the information stored with it, clear shared widget summaries, cancel pending notifications or delete emails already sent to support.

To delete your account and the profile, schedule and saved conversation information stored with it, email daylineappsupport@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will remove it. Backups and provider logs may keep copies for a short time afterward.

Dayline Plus and purchase information

Dayline Plus is an optional subscription managed by Apple. Purchases, renewals and cancellations go through your Apple ID, and Apple processes your payment. Dayline does not receive your card details. The subscription terms are in our Terms of Service. The app also uses Apple's Standard End User License Agreement.

Website analytics

The usedayline.com website uses Microsoft Clarity to collect usage and behavior data, such as page views, clicks and scrolling, to help us understand how visitors use the site. This is separate from the Dayline app. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

If we change what we collect or how we use it, we will update this page and the date below before the change takes effect. For privacy questions or requests, email daylineappsupport@gmail.com.

This policy covers the current Dayline beta. Last updated October 2, 2026.
