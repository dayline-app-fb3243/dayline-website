Meet your day in Dayline.

Review your places, routes and journal in one visual timeline. Use Search in Journal to explore supported, indexed entries in the current beta. Read the current-beta guide.

A little more clarity.
For your whole day.

Review your recorded visits, routes and geolocated journal media in Timeline.

Review your timeline

  1. Open Timeline to see recorded visits, routes and geolocated journal media.
  2. Choose Day, Week, Month or Year to change the period.
  3. Recording depends on your permissions and the location updates delivered by iOS.

Your day.
In your own words.

Search your journal with a question or a voice clip. Results depend on supported, enabled indexing in the current beta.

Search your journal

  1. Open Journal, then tap the magnifying-glass Search control.
  2. Type a question or use the microphone. Available results depend on supported, enabled indexing.
  3. Exiting Search ends the session. Opening a place and returning keeps the session.

The moments.
And the memories.

Add photos or videos to a journal entry. Photo-search indexing needs supported, enabled access and locally available photos.

Choose what to include

  1. Open Journal > + to start a note.
  2. Use + in the editor, then Photos, to choose photos or videos.
  3. Photo-search indexing is not guaranteed in this beta.

Your day stays your business.

Your timeline and journal are stored on your device in this beta. Sign-in and maps use online services.

Know where your data goes

  1. Your journal and saved timeline data are stored locally in this beta.
  2. Sign-in uses an online account service. Maps and place features use Apple services.
  3. The current beta has no ads, third-party tracking or app analytics service.

Set your schedule.

Keep your wake-up time, bedtime, daily steps goal and work hours in your profile.

Set your schedule

  1. Open Profile > Your Schedule.
  2. Set Wake Up and Bedtime.
  3. Set Daily Steps Goal and Work Hours.

Let your day build.

With your permission, Dayline records places and routes. Recording depends on the updates delivered by iOS.

Choose location access

  1. Open Profile > Check Location.
  2. Choose 1 Minute, 5 Minutes or 10 Minutes.
  3. The interval is not an exact recording schedule guarantee. Permissions and iOS delivery affect recording.

Keep a note.
Or say it out loud.

Write a journal entry or save audio. Transcription needs an available on-device recognizer that supports the language.

Write a journal entry

  1. Open Journal > + and write your note.
  2. Use + in the editor for Camera, Photos or Audio. Camera captures photos; choose videos from Photos.
  3. Tap Save. Saved audio may have no transcript.

A few useful tips.

Choose which photos and location data Dayline can access. Change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

Set it up your way

  1. Choose photo and location permissions in iOS Settings.
  2. Photo-search indexing needs supported, enabled access and locally available photos.
  3. Review your location interval in Profile > Check Location.

What stays on your phone?

Your timeline and journal stay local in this beta. Removing local data does not delete your server account or every copy of your data.

Remove local data

  1. Open Profile > Your data.
  2. Tap Delete Local Account Data when signed in, or Delete Data on This iPhone. Confirm Delete.
  3. This is permanent local removal. Read the privacy policy for the full limits.
