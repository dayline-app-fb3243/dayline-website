Your day stays
your business.

Your timeline and journal are stored on your device in this beta. No ads or third-party tracking. Sign-in and maps use online services.

Know where your data goes

  1. Your plans, journal and saved timeline data are stored locally in this beta.
  2. Sign-in uses an online account service. Maps and place features use Apple services.
  3. There are no ads, third-party tracking or an app analytics service in this beta.

Let your day build.

With your permission, Dayline saves location samples and visits, and adds photo references to your timeline. You control access in iOS Settings.

Choose location access

  1. Allow location access for places, routes and routines.
  2. Choose a 1, 5 or 10 minute interval for saved route points.
  3. Change location access in iOS Settings whenever you want.

Keep a note.
Or say it out loud.

Save journal text, recordings or videos. Voice transcription uses on-device recognition. Microphone access is used only while recording.

Keep a journal recording

  1. Record a voice clip when you want to keep a spoken note.
  2. Microphone and speech-recognition permissions support recording and transcription.
  3. Transcription requires on-device recognition and does not fall back to remote speech recognition.

A few useful tips.

Choose which photos Dayline can access. Pick your route-point interval. Change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

Set it up your way

  1. Choose which photos the app can access. On-device photo analysis works with locally available photos.
  2. Pick the route-point interval that suits you.
  3. Review permissions in iOS Settings. You stay in control of access.

What stays on your phone?

Your timeline and journal stay local in this beta. Sign-in, maps and support email use online services. Profile > Your data removes local app data, but does not delete your server account.

Review your choices

  1. Your timeline and journal do not sync to a Dayline server in this beta.
  2. Profile > Your data signs you out and removes local app data.
  3. This local reset does not delete your server account or every copy of your data. Read the privacy policy for the full limits.
