How to use Timeline, Journal, Search and your profile in the current Dayline beta.

Review your timeline

Open Timeline to review saved places and routes. Timeline shows recorded visits, routes and geolocated journal media.

  1. Use Day, Week, Month or Year to change the period.
  2. Recording depends on your permissions and the location updates delivered by iOS.

Search your journal

Open Journal, then tap the magnifying-glass Search control.

  1. Type a question or use the microphone.
  2. Search is a current-beta feature; available results depend on supported, enabled indexing.
  3. Exiting Search ends the session. Opening a place and returning keeps the session.

Photos and videos

You can add photos or videos to a journal entry.

  1. Use Photos in the journal editor to choose photos or videos.
  2. Photo-search indexing needs supported, enabled access and locally available photos. It is not guaranteed in this beta.

Set your schedule

Open Profile > Your Schedule.

  1. Set Wake Up and Bedtime.
  2. Set Daily Steps Goal and Work Hours.

Location and places

Open Profile > Check Location.

  1. Choose 1 Minute, 5 Minutes or 10 Minutes.
  2. Recording depends on permission and iOS delivery. The interval is not an exact recording schedule guarantee.

Write a journal entry

Open Journal > +.

  1. Write your note.
  2. Use + in the editor for Camera, Photos or Audio. Camera starts photo capture; choose videos from Photos.
  3. Tap Save.
  4. Audio transcription needs an available on-device recognizer that supports the language. Saved audio may have no transcript.

Permissions and useful tips

Choose the photos and location access you want Dayline to use in iOS Settings.

  1. Photo-search indexing needs supported, enabled access and locally available photos.
  2. Review location permissions and your interval in Profile > Check Location.
  3. Change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

Remove local data

This is permanent local removal. Open Profile > Your data.

  1. Tap Delete Local Account Data when signed in, or Delete Data on This iPhone.
  2. Confirm Delete.
  3. Local removal does not delete your server account or every copy of your data. Read the privacy policy for the full limits.

This guide covers the current Dayline beta. Features may change as the app develops.
