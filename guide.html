Use your timeline

Add your plans, then use the timeline to see them alongside the moments from your day.

  1. Add the plans you want to keep track of.
  2. Open your timeline to see your plans alongside saved moments.
  3. Use your journal to add your own notes about what happened.

Search your day

Type a question or record a voice clip in Search. The assistant interprets searches with Apple's on-device models where available.

  1. Open Search and type your question, or record a voice clip.
  2. Search interpretation uses Apple's on-device models where available.
  3. Search conversations last for the current session. Closing Search deletes temporary search audio.

Photos and moments

Allow access to the photos and places you want to include. Add journal text, voice clips or videos to keep your own notes about the day.

  1. Allow Dayline to access the photos you choose in iOS Settings.
  2. Photo dates and saved locations help place memories in your day.
  3. Add journal text, voice clips or videos when you want to keep a personal note.

Start with your plans

Add the plans you want to keep track of. Your timeline gives them one place to live, alongside your journal and saved moments.

  1. Start with the plans you want to keep track of today.
  2. Use the visual timeline to keep those plans in one place.
  3. Add journal notes and recordings as the day unfolds.

Location and places

With your permission, Dayline saves location samples and visits, and adds photo references to your timeline. You control access in iOS Settings.

  1. Allow location access for places, routes and routines.
  2. Choose a 1, 5 or 10 minute interval for saved route points.
  3. Change location access in iOS Settings whenever you want.

Journal and voice

Save journal text, recordings or videos. Voice transcription uses on-device recognition. Microphone access is used only while recording.

  1. Record a voice clip when you want to keep a spoken note.
  2. Microphone and speech-recognition permissions support recording and transcription.
  3. Transcription requires on-device recognition and does not fall back to remote speech recognition.

Permissions and useful tips

Choose which photos Dayline can access. Pick your route-point interval. Change permissions any time in iOS Settings.

  1. Choose which photos the app can access. On-device photo analysis works with locally available photos.
  2. Pick the route-point interval that suits you.
  3. Review permissions in iOS Settings. You stay in control of access.

Your data and privacy

Your timeline and journal stay local in this beta. Sign-in, maps and support email use online services. Profile > Your data removes local app data, but does not delete your server account.

  1. Your timeline and journal do not sync to a Dayline server in this beta.
  2. Profile > Your data signs you out and removes local app data.
  3. This local reset does not delete your server account or every copy of your data. Read the privacy policy for the full limits.

This guide covers the current Dayline beta. Features may change as the app develops.
